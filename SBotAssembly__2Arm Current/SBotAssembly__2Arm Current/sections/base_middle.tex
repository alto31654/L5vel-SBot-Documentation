\assemblystep{Install the Horizontal Extrusions}
Attach the extrusions horizontally along the perimeter of the base
between the vertical extrusions. Center at 265mm from the top of the
vertical extrusions and affix with corner brackets underneath the
horizontal extrusion. Secure with Loctite.

\begin{tipbox}
Image needed: side view showing the corner brackets and tape measure.
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Cut the Grid Board}
Cut the grid board to 510mmx510mm. Cut a 25mmx25mm square out of each
corner. Cut a [diameter] hole [dist] from one edge and [dist] from it's adjacent edge. Set aside.

\begin{tipbox}
Image needed: the cut grid board.
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install the Grid Board}
Place 2 T-Nuts on the topside of each horizontal aluminum extrusion. Set
the grid board on top with the [diameter] hole closest to the rear back corner of the Sbot. Secure with M5x12mm bolts and washers.

\begin{tipbox}
The board is in imperial units, so additional holes may need to be
drilled to fully secure it.
\end{tipbox}

\begin{tipbox}
Image needed: the grid board mounted on the base.
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install the PDP}
Place the PDP (Power Distrubution Panel) in the center of the of the grid board and affix with either velco strips or with M5 socket screws, washers, and nuts. Be sure to have the panel oriented parallel with the front edge of the SBot and that the battery connector is facing the rear.

\begin{tipbox}
Image needed: PDP on gridboard
\end{tipbox}

Each of the motor and encoder power cables should be fed through the grid board to reach the PDP. Extend any cables as necessary with additional wire and WAGO connectors. Crimp each cable end before inserting into the PDP. It is recommended that each set of cables go to the corner of the PDP closest to it's respective Swerve Module.

\begin{tipbox}
    Image needed: PDP wired
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install the Meta Quest 3S}
Install the bottom half of the Quest mount to the front of the SBot using M5 socket screws.

\begin{tipbox}
    Image needed: Bottom half of the quest mount
\end{tipbox}

Tie the elastic cord in a loop and place into the groove on the bottom half of the mount before placing the top half on top. Secure with M5 socket screws. Press the Quest onto the front of the mount and pull the cord over the front and around the hook on top.

\begin{tipbox}
    Image needed: 2 images, assembled mount and then with quest on it
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install Quest Adapter}
Secure the Quest adapter with velcro adhesive strips behind the Quest. Plug the USB-C cable into the quest.

\begin{tipbox}
    Image needed: quest adapter
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install the PC}
Place the PC in the front right corner of the gridboard. Lay on its side. The rear should be facing the back of the SBot. Secure with velcro straps. The feet may need to be removed for proper adhesion. 

\begin{tipbox}
    Image needed: pc installed
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install Battery}
Secure the battery in the rear left corner of the SBot with vecro strips. Be sure that the battery connector can easily reach the other end that is attached to the PDP. Secure the breaker switch the the grip board using a M5 socket screw, washers, and nuts.

\begin{tipbox}
    Image Needed: battery installed and connected.
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Connect CANs}
Place the CAN USB and the CANivore on the front left corner of the SBot, and feed the wires through the gridboard. Secure with velcro adhesive strips. Connect a USB to USB-C cable to the CANivore. Connect both modules to a USB expansion hub and afiix with velcro. Connect the other end of the hub to a rear USB port on the PC.

\begin{tipbox}
    Images needed: CANs and hub, back of PC
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install the Ethernet Switch}
Affix the ethernet switch anywhere in the rear right corner. Connect a short ethernet cable between the PC and the swith. Connect a longer cable from the switch to Quest adapter and from the switch to the control box.

\begin{tipbox}
    Images needed: switch with rear of pc, quest switch, arm control box
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Connect Power Cables}
Connect power cables from the power supply to the control box and the PC. If needed, the two screws can be removed and the connector on the PC can be fliiped the other way around. Connect a USB to USB-C cable to the ethernet switch and the dual USB-C adapter plugged into the power supply. Connect a USB-C cable from the Quest adapter and the dual USB-C adapter. 

\begin{tipbox}
    Images needed: photos showing all power cables
\end{tipbox}
