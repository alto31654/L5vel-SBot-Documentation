\assemblystep{Assemble Top Frame}
Connect 4 pieces of the 2020 x 510mm aluminum extrusions using the three directional connectors and fasten with set screws. Place two parallel extrusions [dist] apart and centered in the frame. Use corner brackets, T-Nuts, and M5x10mm buttonhead screws to secure to the frame.

\begin{tipbox}
    Image needed: square frame
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install and Secure Top Frame}
Place the top frame on top of the SBot, making sure to align the prongs of the three directional connectors with the vertical extrusions. Secure with the provided set screws. Install corner brackets at all 8 corners made by the top frame and the sides. Install a corner plate over each corner bracket.

\begin{tipbox}
    Images needed: frame on bot, corner brackets installed, plates installed
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install Bracing}
Install two 2020 extrusion braces in the front and two in the back. The braces will be trapezoidal in shape with one side being [length] long and the other being [length] long. The angle is 45 degrees on both sides. Secure with two 45 degree brackets and 1 135 degree bracket and T-Nuts for each brace.

\begin{tipbox}
    Images needed: simple drawing of brace, braces on front, on back
\end{tipbox}

\assemblystep{Install the Arm}
Align the short edge of the mounting plate with the front edge of the SBot and the long edges with the two extrusions on the top. Secure with T-Nuts and M5x[length] socket screws. Place the arm on top facing the front and secure using the provided screws.

    Images needed: plate attached, arm attached
\end{tipbox}
